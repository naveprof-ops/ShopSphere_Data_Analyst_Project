# Interview Preparation

## 60-second explanation
"I built ShopSphere, an e-commerce analytics project focused on customer retention and revenue performance. I generated a relational dataset covering customers, orders, products, returns and acquisition channels. I used SQL to calculate revenue, AOV, repeat purchase rate, return/cancellation rates, customer lifetime revenue and product performance. I then designed a Power BI star schema with DAX measures for executive KPIs and retention analysis. The final dashboard helps management identify high-value customers, product return issues and operational revenue leakage."

## Questions to prepare
1. Why did you choose this business problem?
2. How did you define revenue?
3. Why exclude cancelled orders from revenue?
4. How did you calculate repeat purchase rate?
5. What is RFM analysis?
6. Why use a star schema?
7. What is a fact table?
8. What is a dimension table?
9. Why create a Date table in Power BI?
10. What is DAX?
11. Difference between calculated column and measure?
12. How did you calculate AOV?
13. How would you validate the dashboard numbers against SQL?
14. How would you handle missing values?
15. How would you investigate a high return rate?
16. What would you do if revenue increased but profit decreased?
17. How would you identify churn?
18. What additional data would make the analysis stronger?
19. How would you productionize the dashboard?
20. What was the biggest limitation of synthetic data?

## Strong interview principle
Never claim that the dashboard caused a business improvement. This is a portfolio simulation. Explain the recommendations as data-driven proposals.
